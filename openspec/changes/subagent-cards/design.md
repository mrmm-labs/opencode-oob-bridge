## Decisions

- **The bridge owns the card, the connector only posts.** The card is a plain push (`title` and `message`) with `own` and `silent`. `own` means: only in the session's own topic; with none, nothing is sent and the send answers undefined. That one rule gives "no card for a headless parent" without a second topic lookup in the bridge.
- **Where a card is made.** On the first event of a child (`execution.started`, a question or a permission), when the session routes (the same decision as every push). The card of a child that has no topic is retried at most every 30 s.
- **Status.** `running` from `execution.started`, `done` from `succeeded`, `error` from `failed`, `idle` from `interrupted`. `waiting` is not stored: it is shown while a question or permission of that child is pending, so it ends when the answer lands. `pendingGauge` (called whenever a prompt is put or changes state) marks the cards to refresh.
- **Description.** The child's session title, without the trailing `(@agent subagent)` OpenCode adds, through `redact`. The last line is the last non-empty line of the child's last `session.text.ended`, trimmed to 160, through `redact`. Both read `(details hidden, open OpenCode)` with `redact: title`.
- **Pace.** A change schedules one edit per card: after 2 s (`subDebounceMs`) and no sooner than 8 s after the card's last edit (`subGapMs`); more changes in between ride on that edit. Text identical to the one shown is not sent. The elapsed time of a running card is shown as under 1 min or whole minutes, so a tick changes the text at most once a minute. The connector's own send budget (`rate`) still applies and merges queued edits of one message. A card has an empty keyboard so the budget does not merge two cards into one message.
- **A failed edit stops the card** (the message may have been deleted by hand); it is logged once and not resent as a new message.
- **Never deleted.** No code path deletes a card. A finished card keeps its last text; a subagent that runs again edits the status and keeps the last text until it has new text. Cards of subagents finished for an hour are forgotten by the bridge, not removed from the chat.
- **Questions and permissions.** A permission that goes to the per-session digest (the default: `permissions.notify` `digest`) is not a message of its own, so it is not a reply; its card shows `waiting` all the same. The push of a question or of a permission posted on its own carries `replyTo`, the card's message, so Telegram shows it as a reply to the card. If the card does not exist yet it is made first, so the reply has a target. Answers use the prompt's own session id (the child), which is how they already reached it.
- **Nesting.** Depth is the number of parents up to the root, read once when the card is made. Depth 1 has no prefix; deeper cards start with one `↳` per level below the first. All cards are in the root's topic.
- **Shared topic.** Unchanged: nothing in this change posts there, and a child never had a post of its own in it.
- **Not verified live.** The calls (a reply to a message, an empty keyboard on a send, edits at this pace) were not run against the real API.
- **Not built.** A switch to turn cards off.
